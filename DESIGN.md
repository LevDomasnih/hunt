# Hunt — design system

Hunt is for friends renting a home abroad together: finding a place, deciding as a group, then living in it. It should feel like **a friend who knows the place**, not a property portal or a dashboard: bright, calm, a bit playful (toy animals, stickers, typed lines), never "AI-generic". Audience ≈ 20–30.

Values live in `crm/public/app.css` (tokens) and `plugins/hunt/regions/<country>/art/theme.yaml` (region colour). When this file and the code disagree, fix one of them; don't invent a third value.

Art of a region, an animal, a goods toy: the skill `plugins/hunt/skills/hunt-art` (rules, renderer, checks).

## Principles
1. **Photo or thing first.** The home, the event, the toy is the hero, one per screen; key numbers sit next to it, not in a table.
2. **One screen, one job.** Search mode is about deciding; home mode is about living. Don't show search blocks once a home is chosen.
3. **Real numbers, human words.** "1,2 м, лучше до 10:00", "через 3 дня 22 млн", not "Surf index 0.78". Dates and money in the user's format (ru: `22 млн`, `23.12`, `сб 2 декабря`).
4. **Blurred = still open, sharp = decided** (a price in a negotiation, a forecast next to a fact).
5. **Thumb-friendly** on a phone: touch targets ≥ 44 px, primary action at the bottom, one primary per screen.
6. **Roles.** The owner sees everything; members don't see the budget cap or the negotiation limit; guests get a reduced nav. Design every screen for the role that sees it.

## Look
- **One solid colour per screen**, no card stacks on a background: region colour for the main screens (Bali `#E8E35C` sun-lime, Kazakhstan `#9ED0F2` sky, Thailand `#FFB65C`), a colour per section (below), warm grey `#E6E4DF` for calm secondary screens, night `#151413` with paper text `#F2F0EA` in the dark theme. Ink `#151413`. Faint dot grid (18 px) + light film grain on every screen.
- **Type:** Inter Tight (Cyrillic): titles 48–64 px weight 800, tracking −0.055em; statements 26–30 px weight 500, tracking −0.035em; body 15–16; tabular numbers. A **giant blurred word** (180–230 px, blur ~0.09em) behind the hero on key screens ("Дом", "Ночь"…), legible as a shape (≥ 1.3:1 vs the ground). Sentence case, no uppercase tracked labels, no periods on labels and buttons.
- **Typing text** with a blinking caret for the one key statement per screen; may cycle 2–3 lines on Home; static under reduced motion.
- **Hero object:** a photo floating with a soft long shadow and a small tilt, or inside a thick black rounded frame (36–44 radius, 12–16 padding).
- **Stickers:** round yellow `#F2E33A` (lime / sky / white) with 1–2 words, rotated ±10–14°, a black outline on lime.
- **Buttons:** black pills (46 px), outline pills, round 46 px icon buttons; nav = row of pills. No FABs.
- **Lists:** no cards; rows with hairlines (`#1514131F`), 18 px semibold title + 13–14 px secondary, trailing arrow / value / status pill.
- **Paper:** tickets with a perforation and receipts with a torn edge come out of a black slot: the one decision of the screen.
- **Living water** = a circle or a pill (surf, sea, pool), WebGL caustics.
- **Over photos:** dark translucent pills and a bottom scrim only.
- **Maps:** MapLibre + OpenFreeMap (dark style in the dark theme); our labels = HTML markers; the region accent is for maps only. On the site the land is a thick toy piece on a sea of the screen colour: since 26.11 a 3D city made by `citymap.py` (blocks cut by the real streets and raised by their buildings, cream / peach / white / pale blue by height, parks with toy trees, a few landmarks, rivers cut), seen from one angle without words; district names are yellow toy signs on a post (HTML, short names from the JSON), river names lie on the water in italic, prices are the usual pins. Not floating chips, not names printed on the ground. District borders are live light walls (`Walls` in `desk.js`, a 2D canvas): a thin white line with a soft turquoise glow and a low wall fading upwards with fine scan bands and a slow light running along; a hint, never louder than the city, no dark lines. Paused off-screen, one still frame under reduced motion.
- **Icons:** lucide, one size per context; no emoji in the chrome (fine in user content).
- **Motion:** springy and short (press 0.96–0.97, sheets 280 ms, swipe fling with rotation), parallax ≤ 10 px on the hero, stickers bob 4 s; nothing loops except grain, water, effects and stickers. Reduced motion → still.

## Code
- **In code (wave 8):** `data-tone` on `<html>` (`region` | `grey` | `night`, set per route in `app.js` `toneFor`; the dark theme always uses the night tokens) picks `--ground`; `theme.yaml` `screen:` → `scripts/copy-art.mjs` → `regions.css` `--screen` (+ `--region-accent` for maps only). Buttons, figures and focus are ink (`--accent*` = ink), focus ring `--focus` `#1F6BFF`. Secondary text is `#46433D` on region screens (≥ 5.6:1 on lime, sky and orange) and `#5F5C56` on grey; attention / done / risk text `#6E3A05` / `#1B5230` / `#7E2316` (≥ 5.2:1 on every screen colour). Dot grid + grain = a fixed CSS layer (`body::before`, SVG noise), no canvas. All WebGL (event effects and the water circles, shader `water`) goes through `crm/public/fx.js`: ≤ 2 animated at once, DPR ≤ 2, paused off-screen, still frames under reduced motion. Pieces: `TopBar`, `BigWord`, `Typed`, `Sticker`, `AniStickers`, `FloatPhoto`, `CountUp` (`app.js`, "wave 8 · Block vibe pieces").
- **Wave 8.1 (after the live review: "grey, boring, dead money, afisha hard to reach"):** home nav = Дом · Афиша · Деньги · Ещё (map and plan at the top of Ещё + quick-link pills on Home; search mode keeps Сегодня · Варианты · Карта · План, Афиша sits in the settings and project menus). Every main tab has its own solid colour (`data-tone`): Дом / search Today = region, Афиша = `afisha` (night palette) or `afisha-day` hot coral `#FF8A5B` 06:00–17:00 local (TH: pink `#FF8FB1`), Деньги = `money` mint `#8EE3C8`, Ещё = `more` sky `#9ED0F2` (KZ: lime); warm grey only for secondary screens. On coral the secondary text is `#36332E` and mango / done / risk `#4A2600` / `#123D22` / `#5E1A0F` (all ≥ 5.1:1); on mint and sky `#46433D`. Empty states are designed, never a grey box or a bare "0": Деньги shows ghost category rows, a "скоро" sticker and the add pill; Афиша shows "Афиша собирается", the next sweep on a sticker and the region's verified recurring events (`events.yaml` `recurring` + `name_ru` / `recurring_ru` / `starts`) as "каждый день" tickets. Афиша has no distance cutoff: district chips ("Рядом" + pack areas, multi-select), far events sink and show the ride time. Home cycles 2–3 facts in the typed line and shows tonight's top event as a black mini-ticket. Agent offline = a small line in the settings sheet and at the bottom of Ещё after 24 h, never a Home banner.
- **Chats after the home is chosen (wave 8.3, `design/mockup-v8/`):** Переписка shows the home only: "Ждут тебя", then "Дом" (the chosen home's chat as "Хозяин" with its photo, house contacts by role with a lucide icon, contacts not tied to a listing), and one row "Архив поиска · N переписок" with "N новое" when an old home wrote again. The archive is calm grey and read only (a lock line instead of "Написать самому", an "архив" chip, no price): wrote after the move / turned down / no answer (collapsed). It never counts as "waiting for you" (rail badge, morning digest); Hunt may answer it with one "we already live in another home" for your yes. Rule in code: `thArchived` (`app.js`) and `ARCHIVED_THREAD` (`notify.ts`).
- **Vinyl toys (wave 8.2):** members' animals ≥ 32 px are the glossy toys (in the pack: `art/toys/<slug>[-full]-192|384.webp`, copied to `crm/public/toys/` by `copy-art.mjs`; made with the skill `hunt-art`) in the disc pop-out sticker: a solid disc in the member's `bg_light` / `bg_dark` with a white ring (0.07× the disc), the toy popping out above the top edge, the body cut by the inner disc. Below 32 px, and if a toy fails to load, the flat SVG stays (`Av` in `app.js`, `.av.toy` in `app.css`). Stickers (`AniStickers`) bob 4 s, still under reduced motion; avatars in rows don't move. **Free-standing (21.11, the human's pick):** on Home stickers, stickers on photos, "who's going" and the reveal the toy stands on its own with a soft contact shadow, no disc (`Av free`, `-full` files); rows, lists and the header keep the disc.
- **Theme:** user setting in Settings → Тема: Светлая (default) / Тёмная / Авто (follows Telegram colour scheme, or the system outside Telegram); stored per device (localStorage `hunt.theme`). In Light the evening variants are off (Home stays the region colour, Afisha uses the daytime coral); in Dark Afisha and the evening Home are night; Auto switches with Telegram/system, Afisha by local time.

## Events
- Photos feel current: real recent photos from the organizer or venue, sharp, no stock, no text-heavy flyers. If none passes the quality bar, use the place photo or a styled category card.
- Tickets: photo + perforation with notches + stub (time, price, one badge, action). Language badge RU / EN / ID or "Без слов". Recurring nights get a "Каждый понедельник" pill.
- One effect per photo, a content moment, never on chrome. Category → effect (data table, extendable): fire shows → embers · sea → caustic light · music/dance → bokeh · surf → foam · volcano/sunrise hikes → mist · yoga/wellness → rays + dust · waterfalls/rivers → droplets · markets/food → steam · temples/ceremonies → incense smoke · cinema → film grain · festivals/kids → confetti · stargazing → stars · jungle at night → fireflies · bars/parties → neon light sweeps · padel/night sport → floodlights · workshops/day sport → none. Recurring nights get a "Каждый понедельник" pill and a weekday strip.
- Shaders in `design/shaders/` (premultiplied alpha); ≤ 2 animated, paused off-screen.

## Orders
- **Orders (wave 10, approved mockups `design/mockup-v5/`: the mix "Прилавок" + "По одному" + "Чек"):** `data-tone` `orders` = lilac `#A98CFF`, `orders-sky` for the fish shop, night in the dark theme; on lilac ink-2 `#2B2350` (5.4:1), attention / done / risk `#432200` / `#0F3319` / `#55160C` (≥ 5.2:1), the blurred word `#7650EE` (sky `#5FA8DB`), focus ring ink. Shop: supplier pills + Услуги, the typed question, up to 8 big toys (two large, then threes, rotated, black round quantity badge, the adder's animal), search, groups with hairline rows. Item: huge toy, the noun as the blurred word ("?" without a price), giant quantity between an outline − and a black + pill, swipe sideways, a strip of neighbours. Basket: a printed receipt (white paper with a torn edge in both themes, coming out of a black slot, toys over its left edge); the same receipt without the slot on the order and in the expense. Bottom: the black cart bar; check and status screens dock two pills. Unknown prices are never summed: "цену узнаём" pills, "+ N позиций без цены". Money to have ready is a range, not a sum (approved `design/mockup-v5/range-sheet.png`): "приготовить", the giant range sharp over a soft haze of itself (lilac / sky, dimmed at night; blurred = still open), the exact bounds and up to 4 rows on the receipt, one line of why on the order; once paid the sum is sharp with the expected range as a scale, the verdict in words (done / attention / risk colour + word) and "Hunt запомнил". Goods toys: `crm/public/goods` (`Good` in `app.js`), from `design/art/goods`; an item without its own toy uses its group's. Home: a black ticket with the scooter while an order is active + outline pills "Заказать" / "Как в прошлый раз".
- **Наборы and clearing (approved `design/mockup-v5/sets-sheet.png`, `png/sets-*`):** the basket receipt has its own tools in the header: "Очистить" (trash icon + word, outline pill on the paper) and under it "Сохранить как набор" (bookmark icon). "Очистить" never acts at once: a sheet "Убрать всё из корзины?" says how many, the range and who added them ("добавляли вы оба"), with "Очистить" (black) / "Оставить" (outline); afterwards the toast "Убрали N позиций" carries "Вернуть", which puts back exactly the same lines, quantities and adders (the server keeps them 15 min). An empty basket offers "Из набора" rows of that supplier ("В корзину") under the usual pills. On the counter the sets are a row of tiles under the question ("Наборы"): each in its supplier's tone (lilac / sky, ink outline) in light, neutral tiles with a supplier-colour dot at night; toys, the name, "по пн" with the repeat icon (or the other supplier's name), N позиций, the range. The set sheet: name + pencil, the toys strip with "+N", the names, "приготовить" and the range (no breakdown), "Напоминать" Нет / Каждую неделю / Раз в 2 недели, weekday circles пн–вс, "Во сколько" (09:00 by default), the line "пн 30.11 в 09:00 Hunt напишет вам обоим в Telegram. Сам заказ не уйдёт…", "В корзину" + a round delete. Saving: the name prefilled by kind ("Фрукты на неделю", "Рыба на ужин", "<supplier>: как обычно"), the summary "<supplier>, N позиций, сейчас <range>", reminder off by default; a taken name is an inline error under the field. "В корзину" adds to what is there (the larger quantity wins). A delivered order docks "Повторить" (exactly that order) + "Сохранить как набор", says "Корзина снова пустая" only when it is, and the receipt is one row into Деньги. The paid label is the expense's real method (наличными / переводом / картой), else "оплатили". The Telegram reminder: "Понедельник: пора заказать «…»", N позиций and the same range, buttons "Собрать корзину" / "Пропустить"; a set never sends an order.

## Desktop site
- **The manager's desk:** a field of one colour, columns without cards, the left menu in pills (changes by stage: search = Сегодня, Поиск, Переписка, План, Деньги, Документы; living = Дом, Переписка, Деньги, Афиша, Заказы, Документы), and Hunt as a black rounded device on the right. The device prints **one white ticket** from its slot: the decision. Black belongs to Hunt and to decisions; a selected row is a light fill.
- Section colours: Дом and Сегодня = region, Переписка = sky, Поиск = sea with a toy land, Деньги = mint, Афиша = coral (night in the dark theme), Заказы = lilac, Документы = warm grey.
- Переписка: rows 60 px in groups (Ждут тебя / Торг / Ждём / Закрыто), home first, then person and channel; Russian large, the original small under it, in bubbles and in the ticket; what Hunt did is a pill inside the feed; the ticket sits under the last message and never covers it; the undo of 8 s appears only after sending. The limit is visible to the owner only.
- Поиск: the right zone is the chosen house, Hunt folds into a black pill with the ticket above it; the ticket never covers the map.
- Text ≥ 14 px (13 for time only), contrast ≥ 4.5:1, status not by colour alone, focus ring `#1F6BFF`, keys J / K, Enter, 1 / 2 / 3, Cmd+K. One caret per screen. Live facts are not repeated next to the ticket that asks about them.
- Breakpoints: < 1024 the mobile layout; 1024–1279 rail 72 px + a bottom black bar "Hunt ждёт тебя"; 1280–1679 rail 200 + device 320; ≥ 1680 device 360. Everything important fits a height of 768–780.
- Sign-in: "Войти через Telegram" (the page shows a number, the bot asks which) or a one-time link from `/web`; a demo without sign-in on invented data; "Поставить свой". No passwords, no e-mail.
- In code: `crm/public/desk.js` + `desk.css` (≥ 1024 px, `data-desk` on `<html>`, tones `desk-sky` / `desk-lime`); screens without their own desktop layout show the phone screen in the middle column, so nothing is missing. `Ticket` is the one place for decisions (drafts, viewings, power, payments), `sendLater` is the undo. The toy land: `art/island[-city].webp`. Demo: `demo.js`, recorded by `scripts/make-demo.mjs` (`public/demo-home`), homes are toys from `design/art/homes`.

## Writing
Russian first (English mirrors it). Short, conversational, no "—" chains in messages to people; numbers with units (`млн`, `мин`, `м`). Never show passport or card numbers, never show the budget cap to non-owners.

## Don'ts
Card stacks, pastel tints, glass, glows, gradients on chrome, purple-blue "AI" palettes, uppercase tracked labels, emoji in the chrome, tables on mobile, text over busy photos without a scrim, hard-coded values that come from data.
